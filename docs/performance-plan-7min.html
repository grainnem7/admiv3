Seven Minutes on the Board

A plan for a seven-minute live piece: Tim plays the board and the iPad, the facilitator runs the laptop. The structure is set in advance so the scenes change on time underneath Tim, and everything Tim does on top is his own.

The piece

The shape: four scenes in seven minutes

Four scenes, one for each scene pad on the iPad. Each one comes in by itself at its time, on the next pass of the loop, so nobody has to remember to press anything. The audience hears the instrument arrive, grow, open up, and come home.

1 · Arrival

Quiet and sparse so the audience can see that a counter makes a sound. One soft loop holds the room while Tim places his first counters, slowly, one at a time.

Loop: BedBand GentleFill 0%Evolve 0%Phrases on

Tim

  • Places two or three melody counters, leaving space between them.
  • Moves one counter up or down a row and listens to the change.

Facilitator

  • Watches the board and the countdown, and does nothing else.
  • Checks the 👆 mark lights on the iPad when Tim touches it.
1:302 min

2 · Build

The groove arrives: bass and drums loops fade in over a pass. Fill starts adding a few notes around Tim's. This is where the piece gets its pulse.

Loops: Bed + Pulse + BeatBand GentleFill 30%Evolve 0%

Tim

  • Adds counters as the beat comes in, including a drum or bass counter, which the band hands over to him.
  • Uses the Fill strip on the iPad to bring more or fewer added notes.
  • Taps Shine on the Loops page when he wants the hook.

Facilitator

  • If Tim plays something good, taps Capture so it's kept in a free slot.
3:302 min 30

3 · Open

The loops step back and the full band and Evolve take over. This is Tim's solo, the loudest and freest part: the sounds change under his hands and he decides when.

No loopsBand FullFill 40%Evolve 50%

Tim

  • Rearranges the board freely.
  • Uses the Evolve strip, New sound and New idea. Taps Keep when he likes one.
  • Can bring back any loop, or the captured one, from the Loops page.

Facilitator

  • At about 5:30, signals "nearly home" to Tim, using whatever signal you agreed in rehearsal.
6:001 min

4 · Home

Back to the opening loop, and the band leaves, so Tim's own counters are clearly in front. At the 30-second cue Tim taps End piece: the texture thins and a final chord rings out.

Loop: BedBand OffFill 0%Evolve 0%End: Final chord, 2 passes

Tim

  • Lifts counters until two or three are left.
  • At the 30 s cue, taps End piece twice on the iPad.
  • Takes his hands off the board and lets the chord ring.

Facilitator

  • If End hasn't been pressed by the 10 s cue, presses End now on the laptop.
  • After the chord has rung out, presses ■ Stop, then Save session.

The times are a guide. With "When I press End" nothing stops by itself, so if the room wants more you can let the Home scene run on, and if a scene isn't working the facilitator can tap Next scene early.

People

Who does what

Tim: the board and the iPad

  • Plays every note the audience hears from the board.
  • On the iPad: Fill and Evolve strips, New idea, New sound and Keep, the loop pads, and End piece.
  • Plays left-handed. Put the iPad on his left and the board within his comfortable reach.
  • Has nothing to hold down or keep still. Every control acts on a touch.

Facilitator: the laptop

  • Starts the piece (▶ Play) and stops it afterwards.
  • Watches the countdown and gives Tim the agreed signal at 5:30.
  • Backup for everything: Capture, Next scene, End now, Cancel the ending, Mute.
  • Does not change the music during the piece unless something goes wrong. The music is Tim's.
Days before

Prepare the loops and scenes

Do all of this while stopped, with Tim, so the loops are his material. Everything saves to his player profile, in the browser and in a copy on the laptop. When it's ready, press ⤓ Back up to a file as well. Make these in Make a piece → 1 · Loops: put counters down, press Save the board here on an empty loop, and type its name. The four scenes

Once the loops are saved, press ✦ Make my plan in Make a piece → 2 · Scenes: it makes these four scenes at these times, using your loops. Then tap each scene to check it against the table (loops, band, Fill, Evolve) and change anything right there. Keep one sound world and one tempo in all four so the piece sounds like one instrument.

Settings

Settings to check before the piece

Make a piece → 3 · Ending & check

  • Length 7:00
  • The clock starts: With Play
  • How it ends: Final chord (or Slow down, if Tim prefers it in rehearsal)
  • The ending takes: 2 passes
  • When it ends: When I press End
  • Loops fade in and out over: 1 pass

Sound tab

  • Sound world: the same one in all four scenes
  • Each counter plays: A phrase
  • Studio mix ticked
  • Groove tab: polyrhythm loop lengths Off, because they fight phrases
  • Fill ideas from Magenta AI: press New idea once while online beforehand so it has downloaded. Without internet it uses Simple rules.

Connections tab → iPad controls (Tim's profile)

  • What the iPad shows: Fill and Evolve strips only, so they're big. New idea, New sound and Keep pads. Untick Mute so it can't be hit by accident.
  • Speed hidden or 🔒 locked: the tempo stays fixed for the piece.
  • A touch on a strip: Follows the movement, so a rough landing changes nothing.
  • Learn reach with Tim at the venue, in the seat he'll play from.

How long is a pass, and how long does the ending take?

One step on the board is one beat, so a pass is steps × 60 ÷ tempo seconds. Scenes, loop starts and the ending all land on pass boundaries.

Before the day

Three rehearsals

First

Explore, no clock

Go to each scene with Go and let Tim play in it for a few minutes. Find out which loops he likes, whether Final chord or Slow down feels better as an ending, and how much Fill and Evolve he enjoys. Adjust the scenes and save them again.

Second

Full run with the clock

Play all seven minutes with the scenes at their times. Agree the "nearly home" signal for 5:30 (a touch on the arm, a nod, a card). Practise End piece at the 30 s cue. Afterwards, Save session and look at what happened.

Dress

In the room, the whole chain

Same laptop, iPad, camera, lighting, speakers and seat as the performance. Do the setup below from scratch, then a full run. Note the iPad link and the camera position so the day is a repeat.

Contingency

If something goes wrong

The music never stops by itself, so take your time. The audience can't tell a pause from a choice.

What you noticeWhat to do
iPad and laptop both say "waiting"Two servers are running. Close every ADMI terminal, start one with npm run show, and reopen both pages using the same code.
iPad shows but doesn't reactLook for the 👆 mark when Tim touches it. If there's no mark, reload the iPad page. If there's a mark but nothing changes, check the strip isn't 🔒 locked or hidden for this player.
A counter isn't heardNudge it to the centre of its square. If a hand or sleeve is over it, it's held and will come back when the hand moves. As a last resort: Colours → lower Piece coverage.
Counters knocked overDo nothing. The pattern carries on as ghosts (↺) until the counters are put back.
Board bumpedA small nudge is followed automatically. A big move asks for the board again, so keep the loops running and re-find the board.
Too busy or too loudTim brings the Fill strip down. The facilitator taps Stop all loops (two taps on the iPad).
A scene isn't workingTap Next scene, or Go on the one you want. Deleted one by mistake? Undo appears for 15 seconds.
Red banner: "The camera has stopped"The music carries on with the last pattern. Press Try the camera again; check the USB cable.
Stop pressed by accidentWhile the clock runs, the first press only says "Press again to stop" — just don't press again.
End pressed too earlyCancel the ending on the laptop.
Something awfulMute on the laptop, which stops the sound without losing anything. Unmute when ready.
Loops or scenes missingCheck the address is localhost:3001 and Tim is picked on Who's playing. Still missing: Make a piece → 3 → ⤒ Restore from a file with your backup, or a file from .admi-data/backups in the ADMIv3 folder.
Magenta not respondingFill falls back to Simple rules by itself. Carry on.
