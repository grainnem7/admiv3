ADMI · Board Sequencer · redesign proposal

From "where is everything?" to a journey you can follow

A clickable mock-up of the redesigned Board Sequencer, stage by stage, from opening it with Tim to the bow at 7:00. It is built from four reviews: an audit of today's app, comparable loop instruments (Ableton Note and Move, Koala, Circuit, Logic Live Loops) and accessible instruments (Clarion, Soundbeam, Skoog, AUMI), and an inclusive-design review.

What changed

  • Set up becomes a check, not a maze: one automatic run, and you only open a step when something fails.
  • Each missing piece names its fallback: no camera → laptop board; no iPad → keys N/E; no Ableton → ADMI's sound; no cap → volume and speed stay put.
  • The Ready check moves here, to the start, instead of step 3 of making a piece.

Fixes: Set up isn't a gate; iPad, Ableton and cap had no checklist.

What changed

  • Play never starts the piece. Here the music just plays; the clock only exists in Rehearse and Perform.
  • Keep is capture after the fact (as Ableton Note and Move do): no arming, no slot to choose, no typing. Rename later.
  • The loops are a row of tiles with a picture of each, right under the board, and tapping one plays it.
  • "Loop" means one thing only: a kept board.

Fixes: ▶ Play = performance; "Save the board here" 540px away; six meanings of loop.

What changed

  • "Scenes" become sections (a musician's word) on a timeline you can see, ending included as the last block.
  • A section shows three things; the rest is under "More" and defaults to "same as before".
  • A section remembers the whole sound you set (today a scene silently drops Energy, Space, Groove and per-colour sounds).

Fixes: scenes drop settings; 8 parameters per scene; structure hidden in a list.

What changed

  • Perform is a place, not a header button: entering it locks every edit.
  • Two screens: the laptop shows control; the projector and the iPad show only the music. Cues and errors never reach Tim's screen.

Fixes: Show mixed with editing; status and debug text in the performance space.
