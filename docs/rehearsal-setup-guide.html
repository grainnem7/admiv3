Rehearsal Setup Guide

Three rehearsals, one step at a time, with a picture of each screen. By the end the loops are made with Tim, the 7-minute piece is built, the iPad is set up for him, and you've done a full run.

  • After each rehearsal

The pictures use the laptop board (squares clicked on screen). With the real board you put the counters on the board instead; everything else is the same.

First: the three buttons at the top

  • Play: just play the board. Sound, Groove, Board and Connections are here. Nothing else is needed: no loops, no scenes.
  • Make a piece: three steps, 1 Loops, 2 Scenes and 3 Ending & check, all on one panel. Each step is optional, and you can open any step at any time.
  • ▶ Show: for the performance. The board fills the screen with the clock and "Now / Next", and only the buttons you need during the piece.

To start ADMI: in a terminal in the ADMIv3 folder, type npm run show. Open http://localhost:3001 in Chrome, press Board Sequencer, and pick Tim. With the real board, do ⚙ Set up as usual, with the colours' jobs: yellow chords, blue bass, green drums, red melody.

Rehearsal 1 · about 45 minutes

Make the loops with Tim

Each loop is one board that Tim makes, saved once. It then plays on its own.

LoopNameTim puts downUsed for
1Bed2–3 yellow counters, in the lower halfCalm chords at the start and the end
2Pulse1–2 blue counters, lowThe bassline
3Beat1 green counter in a middle rowA medium drum groove
4Beat+1 green counter near the topA busier groove
5Shine3–4 red counters at different heightsA tune Tim can bring in
Step 1

Press "Make a piece"

Press Make a piece at the top. The right side becomes the three steps, starting on 1 · Loops, which has eight empty loops.

Each empty loop has a Save the board here button.

Step 2

Tim puts the counters down and listens

For Bed, Tim puts two or three yellow counters in the lower half of the board.

To hear it, press ▶ Play at the top right. Higher rows are higher notes, and left to right is time. Move counters until he likes it.

Step 3

Save it, and name it

  1. On loop 1, press Save the board here.
  2. Type the name in the box: Bed.
  3. Lift the counters off the board. On the laptop board, press Clear the board.

Loop 1 now shows its name, how many notes it has, and ▶ Play, Replace, ✎ (draw) and ✕ (clear).

Step 4

Make the other four the same way

Counters down, listen, Save the board here on the next empty loop, name it, clear. Use the table above for Pulse, Beat, Beat+ and Shine.

  • To hear a loop on its own: clear the board, press ▶ Play, then press that loop's ▶ Play. It comes in on the next time round.
  • To redo a loop: put the new counters down, press Replace, then press it again to confirm.
  • To fix one note without the board: press ✎ to draw on screen.

Leave loops 6 to 8 empty. During the piece, Capture saves into them.

End of rehearsal 1: open step 3 · Ending & check and press ⤓ Back up to a file. Keep the file on a USB stick.

Rehearsal 2 · about 30 minutes

Build the 7-minute piece

Scenes change the loops and the sound at set times while Tim plays on top. The plan has four scenes: Arrival at 0:00, Build at 1:30, Open at 3:30 and Home at 6:00.

Step 5

Make my plan

Press Next: Scenes → (or 2 · Scenes at the top), then press ✦ Make my plan.

Four coloured scenes across 7:00, using your loops: Arrival plays Bed; Build adds Pulse and Beat; Open is Tim's solo, with no loops; Home brings Bed back.

Every scene has ▶ From here. While stopped, it plays the piece from that scene's time, so you can rehearse one part without playing seven minutes. When you stop, Tim's own settings come back.

Step 6

Change a scene if you want to

Tap a scene's name to open it. You can change:

  • which loops play: tap to tick or untick;
  • the time it comes in, written like 1:30;
  • the sound world, the band, Fill, Evolve and the tempo.

Tap the name again to close it.

Keep the same sound world and tempo in every scene, so the piece sounds like one instrument.

Step 7

Choose the ending, and check

Press Next: Ending & check →, then:

  • Length of the piece: 7:00.
  • How it ends: tap one. Final chord is a clear end; try Slow down with Tim too.
  • The ending takes 2 passes. When it ends: When I press End.

The Ready check below lists what's ready (✓), what to check (!) and what to fix (✕).

In the picture, the three "!" are expected while testing on the laptop board. On the day, with the real board, npm run show and a backup made, they turn to ✓.

Rehearsal 3 · about 45 minutes

The iPad for Tim, then a full run

Step 8

Set up the iPad

  1. Press Play at the top, then the Connections tab, and tick iPad controls.
  2. Open the link it shows in Safari on the iPad; it needs to be on the same Wi-Fi.
  3. Also on the Play page: tick End piece, and Shine if he brings it in.
  4. A pad acts: start with When the finger lands. If pads get pressed by accident, switch to When the finger lifts.
  5. Learn reach: press it, have Tim slide each strip as far as is comfortable, then press Done.

If it says "The iPad can't reach this laptop yet", start ADMI again with npm run show.

Step 9

What Tim sees

The clock, Now / Next, a beat light, his strips and pads, and a big End piece, which needs two taps.

The 👆 mark at the top left lights whenever the iPad is touched, so you can tell "not touched" from "not working".

Step 10

Show, and the full run

Press ▶ Show at the top. The board fills the screen. Press ▶ Start the piece.

Step 11

During the piece

  • The top shows the time left, Now and Next, and the loops playing.
  • Scenes change by themselves. Playing loops show on the board as rings.
  • Cues at 1 minute, 30 seconds and 10 seconds show large. At 30 seconds, Tim taps End piece twice, or you press ⏹ End.
  • When the chord has rung out it stops by itself. Press Leave Show, then Save session.

Stop needs two presses during the piece, so one stray tap can't end it. Cancel the ending brings the music back if End was pressed too soon.

After each rehearsal

  • ⤓ Back up to a file (Make a piece, step 3), and copy it off the laptop.
  • Save session and CSV if it was a run you want for the research.
  • Note what Tim liked and what felt wrong. Next time: Replace a loop, tap a scene to change it, or change an iPad setting.
