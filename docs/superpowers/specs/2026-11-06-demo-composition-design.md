# The demo piece, composed: "Rising Light"

Owner's brief (2026-11-06): the demo is boring and its instruments sound bad. Lean on pads
more than instruments. Think like a composer and make something brilliant that engages an
audience. Keep using Magenta. The plan to build it is
`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-06-demo-composition-plan.md`, carried out by Claude Code
(on the show laptop, in VS Code) and Claude Desktop (in Ableton).

## What went wrong before, in a composer's words

- **No idea to follow.** The loops were patterns, not a theme. An audience holds on to a
  short, memorable melody that comes back changed; there was none.
- **No journey.** Instruments arrived one by one and then took turns. There was no tension
  and release: no hush before a return, no climb to a peak, no moment the room waits for.
- **The wrong sounds for the job.** Solo sampled trombone, clarinet and guitar, played by
  a sequencer, sound like a sequencer. Pads, a soft piano, synth pulses and a cinematic kit
  sound like what they are and blend.
- **Pop harmony.** I V vi IV and its rotations (vi IV I V, and i VI III VII in minor are the
  same four chords) read as cheesy at once.

## The idea

Cinematic, ambient, post-rock in shape (think of Olafur Arnalds, Jon Hopkins, Sigur Ros):
a single four-note theme born in the dark, carried by a pulse, rising to a summit and
resolving into light. Minor all the way until the last minute, when it turns major (a
Picardy third): the payoff the whole piece has been waiting for.

- **Key:** F sharp minor (natural minor), the key note F#4 on the bottom row; the last
  section in F sharp major.
- **Tempo:** 87 BPM; a pass is 2 bars (8 beats, about 5.5 s). It already lines the ending up
  with 7:00 exactly.
- **Length:** 7:00, ten scenes, ending with the Final chord (the texture thins, then the
  tonic chord, F sharp major, rings with a soft crash).

## The theme (the hook)

Four notes, a falling sigh and a reach upwards: **C#, B, A, then up to E.**

Written on the 8 steps of a pass (degree 0 = F#4, 1 = G#4, 2 = A4, 3 = B4, 4 = C#5,
5 = D5, 6 = E5, 7 = F#5):

| Step | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Question (every pass) | C# (4) held 3 beats | | | B (3) | A (2) held 2 beats | | E (6) held 2 beats | |
| Answer (every other pass, added) | | | | | | B (3) | | F#' (7) |

So the first pass asks (C#, B, A, E, left open), the second answers (C#, B, A, B, E, F#,
falling back to C# as the next pass begins). Every strong beat is a note of every chord it
meets (checked by the harmony test).

The theme's journey: a distant fragment on glass bells (Dawn), then whole on a soft felt
piano (Theme), carried over the pulse, alone in the hush, on a warm synth lead an octave up
at the summit, and finally in F sharp major (A sharp and E sharp instead of A and E) in the
afterglow: the same four notes, now hopeful.

## Harmony: two progressions, no pop

Each chord is held 4 bars (2 passes) unless marked "moving" (2 bars). Pads voice every chord
with its colours (ninths, sevenths, suspensions), never as a plain triad.

- **Tide** (the night): i add9, VI maj7, iv m9, i add9. In F sharp minor: F#m(add9), Dmaj7,
  Bm9, F#m(add9). The minor plagal pull (iv to i) is dark and tender, not pop.
- **Rise** (the climb): VI maj7, VII sus4, i add9, i add9. Dmaj7, Esus4, F#m(add9). The
  Aeolian rise, VI VII i: the film climb, lifting to the home chord twice.
- **Drone**: F#m with its fifth, for the dawn.
- **Float** in F sharp major, for the afterglow: F#(add9), then D#m(add9), each 4 bars.

The band's bass holds a **pedal F#** under Tide in the early scenes (the chords move above a
bass that does not), and follows the roots from Heartbeat on.

## The parts (and where each sounds in Ableton)

| Part | Job and ADMI sound | Ableton track | What it plays |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pads | the band's wide pads | Band chords (11) | every chord, two layers, leading |
| Sub | the band's bass | Band bass (12) | pedal F#, then roots |
| Bells | melody, Bells (synth:bell), an octave up | Melody (1) | the theme's fragment: C# and A, held |
| Felt piano | melody, Piano | Melody (1) | the theme, question and answer |
| Soft lead | melody, Synth keys, (an octave up at the summit) | Melody (1) | the theme, singing |
| Pulse | bass job, Synth pluck, an octave up, over the bass | Bass (2) | eighth notes, an ostinato on F# |
| Swells | chord, Soft synth pad, follows the pads, held a pass | Chords (3) | one swell a pass |
| Pulse chords | chord, Wide synth chords, follows the pads | Chords (3) | rhythmic chords, 1, the and of 2, 4 |
| Drums | drums, one kit piece per counter | Drums (10) | half time, cinematic |
| AI sparkles | Magenta's extra notes, frozen | Extra notes (13) | answers around the theme |

Only one instrument per Ableton track sounds at a time, so each scene chooses one melody
sound, one Bass track sound and one Chords track sound (ADMI switches the track's chain on
the pass: loops play their own instrument).

### The pulse (eighth notes, written as two counters a step, the second half a beat late)

Degrees as above, an octave up from the bass register (so F#3 to F#4):

| Beat | 1 | and | 2 | and | 3 | and | 4 | and |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Note | F# (0) | C# (4) | A (2) | E (6) | F# (0) | C# (4) | A (2) | B (3) |

The same for the second bar. On the strong beats it is always F#: a pedal that every
chord of Tide and Rise welcomes. The quarter-note version for the afterglow plays only F#,
C#, A#, C# on beats 1 to 4.

### The drums (half time: the snare on beat 3 of each bar)

- **Heartbeat**: kick on 1 and on the and of 2; rim on 3 of each bar; soft hats on every
  "and" (eight a pass).
- **Build**: as Heartbeat, plus a kick on 3 of bar 2, the rim becomes a snare, hats on every
  eighth, and floor toms on the last beat of every other pass.
- **Summit**: kick on 1, the and of 2, 3, the and of 4; snare on 3 of each bar; hats on every
  eighth; a crash on the first beat of every other pass.

## The form (ten scenes, every change on a pass that is a multiple of four)

| Pass | Time | Scene | What happens | Chords | Energy | Way in |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0:00 | Dawn | the pads rise out of silence on a drone; glass bells breathe the theme's first two notes | Drone | 1 | cut |
| 8 | 0:44 | Theme | the felt piano states the theme; the sub holds F# under the pads | Tide | 2 | fade 1 |
| 16 | 1:28 | Pulse | the pulse begins underneath; the piano answers itself; Magenta's first sparkles | Tide | 2 | cut |
| 24 | 2:12 | Heartbeat | a rising lead in; the drums enter, half time; the swells breathe with the kick; the bass follows the chords | Tide | 3 | cut, lead in: rising |
| 32 | 2:56 | Build | chords move every two bars (Rise); snare, toms, pulse chords driving; more sparkles | Rise moving | 4 | cut |
| 36 | 3:18 | Hush | everything stops but the pads and the piano, alone with the theme | Tide | 1 | cut |
| 40 | 3:40 | Ascent | a drum fill and the pulse and drums return; the theme on the soft lead | Rise | 3 | cut, lead in: drum fill |
| 48 | 4:24 | Lift | Build's drums and pulse chords under the lead | Rise | 4 | cut |
| 56 | 5:08 | Summit | the lead an octave up, the summit kit, everything, Magenta at its busiest | Rise moving | 5 | cut, lead in: drum fill |
| 64 | 5:52 | Afterglow | F sharp major: the piano plays the theme in major over a quiet pulse and warm pads | Float (major) | 2 | cut |
| 72 | 6:37 | the Final chord | the texture thins, F sharp major rings with a soft crash, silent by 7:00 | | | |

The shape: dark and close for two minutes, a body arriving at 2:12, a first climb, the
hush at 3:18 (the moment the room leans in), the long climb back to the summit at 5:08,
then light.

## Magenta

The AI's extra notes are kept, and frozen. Magenta can't be told to play the same twice, so:
it improvises in a rehearsal, ADMI keeps each scene's ideas, the owner listens and keeps the
best, and those ideas are written into the demo. From then on every performance plays the
same AI ideas, the ones chosen by ear. (Today a kept idea is matched against everything on
the board, the player's colours included, so ideas kept with one player would not replay
for another: the plan changes that for loops alone.)

## Tim's part, live

The demo plays by itself; Tim performs over it.

- **The cap, up and down: Tone** (brightness, the filter on every track in Ableton). From
  Heartbeat, Tim opens the sound as it climbs: the audience sees his head lift the music.
- **The cap, left and right: Extra notes** (how much Magenta adds). Or Speed, as now, if
  the owner prefers: then keep its range narrow (about 80 to 95 BPM), as scenes come in by
  the clock and a big speed change moves them off the fills.
- **His counters** play the theme's notes over everything, in the key; in the Hush they are
  the only thing besides the piano.
- **The iPad**: New idea (a fresh Magenta idea, his choice live), End piece.
