# Rising Light, version 4: the later two thirds

Owner, 7 November 2026: "it started off really well and is interesting but the later two thirds
of the piece are really boring and samey. I think it's because we maxed out the 16 loops."

## What was wrong

From the Intrigue (2:12) on, the scenes reused earlier material nearly unchanged:

- Groove II had exactly the loops of the Winds hook.
- The Summit had exactly the loops of the Drop.
- The Final ascent was the Drop with a snare roll added.
- There was one tune in the whole piece, the winds' theme, heard in five scenes.

The sixteen loops were all used, but the sameness came from the arrangement more than the count.

## Version 4, within sixteen loops

Two places freed:

- **Heartbeat**: the breakdown now has no drums at all. After the double time drop, silence from
  the kit is a far stronger contrast than a soft heartbeat.
- **Snare roll** (the slow half): the Tension keeps the fast half, which rolls in its second half
  (passes 3 and 4 of every four), louder each pass. The build is still there; it starts later.

Two new tunes in their places:

- **Harp song** (melody track, harp): the second tune. Where the theme falls (C#, B, A, E), this
  rises (A, C#, E to a held F#) and leans back; it grows over four passes. Heard alone over the
  pads in the breakdown (in F sharp minor: F#, B, C#, E), carried through the rebuild, and bright
  in A major over Groove II.
- **Brass call** (Bass track, trombone over the band's bass): the summit's own, heroic call: key
  note, fifth, the octave held; then an answer from the 2nd pass.

The later scenes:

| Scene | Before | Version 4 |
|---|---|---|
| Breakdown | heartbeat, winds' theme | no drums; the harp song alone over the pads |
| Rebuild | rebuild kit, toms, brass fifths, guitar | the same, and the harp song |
| Groove II | the Winds hook again | the groove band with the harp song, bright |
| Tension | half time, both snare rolls, brass, guitar | half time, the fast roll, brass, guitar, the bells in threes back over the pedal |
| Summit | the Drop again | double time, the brass call, the bells answering, the guitar |
| Final ascent | the Drop and a roll | the winds' theme back high over the brass call, new chords (glow), the roll |

No later scene repeats an earlier scene's loops (tested). The theme is saved for the end, so its
return in the final ascent is a homecoming. Keys, timings, energy, lead ins and Magenta's frozen
ideas are unchanged, so the opening the owner liked plays exactly as before.

Every written note on a strong beat was checked against the chord the pads hold in its scene and
key (the existing test), and each scene still has one sound per Ableton track. The harp on the
Melody track needs the harp chain in the Ableton template (it was there for version 1).
