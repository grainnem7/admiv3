# "Rising Light", version 3: grooves, intrigue and a goal

Builds on version 2 (`2026-11-06-demo-composition-v2-design.md`) and its plan, whose engine
work (sixteen scenes, loops that grow over four passes, the Glow sequence, the fingerpicked
roll, Tim's glow on channel 14, Magenta developing every four passes) it uses. The plan:
`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-06-demo-composition-v3-plan.md`.

## The owner's notes on version 2 (2026-11-06)

A lot better. But it is dreamy now: it needs **upbeat, dramatic and exciting sections with
drums**, more **intrigue** and more **direction**.

## What a composer adds

- **Contrast of tempo feel without changing the tempo.** The piece stays at 87 BPM (the
  scene timing and the ending depend on it), but the drums can feel slow (half time: the
  snare once a bar), normal (a groove: the snare on 2 and 4) or fast (double time: sixteenth
  hats, a busy kick, the snare on every beat). Moving between them is the biggest lever on
  excitement there is.
- **A driving bass.** In the grooves the band's bass plays eighth notes on the root,
  pumping, instead of holding it. Nothing makes music feel upbeat faster.
- **Intrigue: tension that the ear wants resolved.** A pedal note held under moving chords;
  a repeating figure in threes against the fours (3 + 3 + 2 eighths); a dominant pedal (the
  fifth of the coming key) under a long build, so the arrival of the summit lands as a
  resolution, not just a change.
- **Surprise.** A stop: everything cuts out for the last two beats before a big section,
  then crashes back in. A snare roll that doubles in speed over four passes (eighths, then
  sixteenths) into the drop. A crash on the first beat of each new section.
- **Direction.** Every section has a job and points at the next: the opening asks, the
  grooves move, the intrigue section winds the spring, the drop releases it, the breakdown
  catches breath, the second groove runs, the tension section aims straight at the summit,
  the summit arrives, the Big finish ends it.

## The drum patterns (2 bars, beats counted 0 to 8 in quarter beats; 0.5 = an eighth late)

- **Tribal** (the opening): kick 0 and 4; tom 1.5, 2.75, 3.5, 5.5, 6.75, 7.5; rim 3 and 7;
  shaker every eighth from the 2nd pass of four (`passes: [1, 2, 3]`), very soft.
- **Groove**: kick 0, 1.5, 2.75, 4, 5.5, 6.5; snare 1, 3, 5, 7 (the backbeat); hat every
  eighth, ghost sixteenths (0.25 off) on the 2nd and 4th passes; clap with the snare on the
  3rd and 4th; crash on 0 of the scene's 1st pass; a tom fill over beats 6 to 8 of the 4th
  pass (6, 6.5, 7, 7.25, 7.5, 7.75).
- **Half time**: kick 0 and 3.5; snare 2 and 6; toms in threes: 0.75, 1.5, 2.25 ... (every
  three eighths, the 3 + 3 + 2 figure); hat on the beats.
- **Double time**: kick 0, 0.75, 2, 2.5, 4, 4.75, 6, 6.5; snare 1, 3, 5, 7 and ghosts at
  2.75 and 6.75; hats every sixteenth (accents on the eighths); crash on 0 of the 1st and
  3rd passes; a tom run down over beats 6 to 8 of the 4th pass.
- **Heartbeat**: version 1's, soft. **Rebuild**: heartbeat on pass 1, + hats on 2, + snare on
  3, + the groove's fill on 4 (`passes`).
- **Snare roll** (the Tension and the end of the Intrigue): eighths over bar 2 on pass 1,
  eighths all through on pass 2, sixteenths over bar 2 on pass 3, sixteenths all through on
  pass 4, louder each pass (velocity 0.3 rising to 0.65), toms on the last two beats.

Percussion is still warm in sound (Ableton), but in the grooves and the drop it is allowed
to be present and exciting: the ambient rule "nothing hits" now applies to the dreamy
sections only.

## The pitched parts in the new sections

- **Driving bass** (the band's bass, a new "drive" job): the chord's root in eighth notes,
  the first of each bar accented, in the grooves, the drop, Groove II and the summit.
- **Guitar picking**: version 2's picked roll on every eighth (two counters a step) in the
  grooves; the roll on every beat elsewhere.
- **Brass**: held pads in the opening; in the grooves a swell on the first beat of every bar
  (held 4 beats); the pedal F# held through the Intrigue and the Tension.
- **Winds**: the theme as in version 2, growing over four passes; an octave up in the drop
  and the summit.
- **Bells**: a figure in threes (C#, B, A repeating every three eighths) in the Intrigue,
  against the fours: the sound of intrigue.

## Stops

A new lead in, **Stop**: the last two beats before the scene lands are silent (loops, band,
extra notes), then the scene comes in with its crash. Used into the Intrigue, Groove II and
the Summit.
